About CareMate Plants

CareMate Plants is a general-purpose plant-care utility for keeping plant records, care schedules, photos, and a journal. This notice explains how the current app and this privacy website handle information.

The app has no accounts or login, no developer-operated backend for plant data, and no cloud sync of plants. It has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or premium tier. It uses local notifications, optional Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics for diagnostics, and Yandex banner advertising.

Information Stored on Your Device

Plant records, plant names, textual location tags, care settings, completion history, journal notes, selected photo copies, and app preferences are stored locally. CareMate does not operate a service that uploads or synchronizes this content to its own servers.

Local content is distinct from technical information processed by third-party SDKs and from information shown to the operating system for notifications. The sections below explain these other uses; local storage does not mean that every kind of information stays on the device.

Plant Photos

When you choose a plant photo through a gallery or camera flow available on your device, CareMate stores an app-owned local copy for that plant. The app has no developer-operated photo-upload service.

A selected or copied image may retain existing metadata, including EXIF information and GPS coordinates previously embedded by a camera or another app. CareMate does not guarantee that this metadata is removed. This is separate from the app requesting your current device location.

Replacing a photo or deleting its plant triggers cleanup of the previous app-owned copy. This does not delete gallery originals or guarantee removal of copies or caches held by a picker, camera, or other provider.

Notifications

Care reminders are scheduled locally with the operating system. CareMate does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging or a remote push-notification service.

To display a reminder, the operating system receives notification content that can include the plant name, care name or custom care text, and textual location tag. Visibility on the lock screen and in notification history depends on your operating-system settings. You can manage notification permission and enable or disable reminders for individual care tasks.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics is optional and helps us understand app usage and improve the product. CareMate keeps Analytics collection disabled and blocks app-defined product events until you grant the Analytics choice. Unknown or denied choices do not permit these events. You can change the choice in the privacy area of Settings.

App-defined analytics use limited values such as counts, predefined categories, app language, notification permission state, and coarse care timing, status, or activity. They do not include raw plant names, journal text, custom care text, plant photos, photo paths, or textual location tags.

When Analytics is enabled, the SDK may also process technical app, device, network, and usage metadata and identifiers under Google's policies. CareMate keeps Google's advertising-storage, advertising-user-data, and advertising-personalization consent settings denied. This does not make Firebase Analytics anonymous or mean that Firebase Core and all supporting SDKs have no technical activity before a choice.

Crash Reporting

Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose failures and improve reliability. It operates independently of your Analytics choice; denying Analytics does not disable crash diagnostics.

Crash reporting may process technical stack traces, error categories, application state, app and device information, and installation or session metadata. CareMate's app-defined Dart error reports are sanitized to avoid sending raw plant names, location tags, journal notes, custom care text, or photo paths.

Native crash reporting and supporting SDKs may collect their own technical metadata. Sanitization of app-defined reports is not a guarantee that every possible native diagnostic contains no user information.

Advertising

The Android app displays banner ads using Yandex Mobile Ads. CareMate passes your Advertising choice to the SDK before its explicit initialization and applies later changes. New app-issued ad requests remain blocked while the native choice is unconfirmed.

Yandex advertising technology may process technical app, device and network information, device or advertising identifiers where available, delivery and measurement signals, and ad interactions such as impressions and clicks. Identifier availability and use depend on the SDK, platform, and your device privacy controls.

Denying or withdrawing Advertising consent does not guarantee zero processing by the advertising technology. Ads may still be shown after you deny or withdraw the Advertising choice where permitted by the SDK and service configuration. The Advertising choice is not an ad-free setting. CareMate does not promise a particular advertising mode.

Yandex advertising technology may include supporting measurement components, including AppMetrica. CareMate does not offer or directly initialize AppMetrica as a separate app-analytics feature.

Location Information

A plant's location, such as Bedroom, Office, or Balcony, is a text tag you enter. It is not GPS location. The Android app does not request precise or approximate location permission or use a GPS location API, and Yandex location tracking is disabled in the app's SDK configuration.

Third-party services may nevertheless derive a region or approximate location from an IP address or other network signals. A photo may also contain location metadata as explained above. These possibilities are separate from reading your current GPS location.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local content is generally kept until you edit or delete it, remove app storage, or uninstall the app, subject to platform and device behavior. The app provides these individual controls:

  • Deleting a plant removes it from the active local collection with its current care and journal data, and triggers cleanup of its app-owned photo.
  • Deleting a care task removes its care configuration; historical journal entries may remain as history.
  • Deleting a journal entry removes the selected entry. Editing replaces the corresponding active content.

If saved data cannot be read, the app may preserve a local recovery copy to protect against data loss. Starting fresh or adding new plants does not erase this copy. Recovery copies currently have no automatic expiration and no separate in-app deletion control.

There is no account-deletion flow because there are no accounts, and no current in-app 'delete all local data' button. Android's app-storage controls or uninstalling usually remove local application data, including recovery copies, subject to platform and device behavior. This is not a secure-erasure guarantee and does not remove gallery originals or other providers' copies.

CareMate's Android configuration disables app-data cloud backup and device-transfer backup. Platform and device-manufacturer behavior can vary. This statement is specific to Android.

Deleting a plant, care task, journal entry, or local app data does not erase information already processed by third-party services. Those services retain and process information according to their own policies and service configurations.

Data Security

CareMate uses application-private storage where applicable and limits data included in app-defined telemetry, including sanitized error reports. Third-party network services use their own security mechanisms.

No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete protection. This notice does not promise app-level encryption at rest or secure erasure.

Your Choices

The initial privacy screen appears before onboarding. Analytics and Advertising are independent choices, and your choices are saved. You can review or change them later in Settings, in the privacy area.

  • Allow optional data grants both choices; Use only necessary denies both. Manage choices lets you allow one and deny the other.
  • Deny or withdraw Analytics to stop app-defined product events and disable Analytics collection. Crash diagnostics remain independent.
  • Change Advertising consent to update the choice passed to Yandex. This does not remove all ads. If applying a choice fails, it remains saved and can be retried; new app-issued ad requests remain blocked until application is confirmed.
  • Use system notification settings and individual care-task reminder switches to control notifications.
  • Edit or delete plant, care, and journal records, choose or replace a photo, or use your operating system's app-storage and uninstall controls.
  • Use your device's advertising/privacy controls for identifiers where those controls are available.

Audience

CareMate Plants is a general-purpose plant-care utility. This Privacy Policy does not establish a separate age restriction.
